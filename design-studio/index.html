    How It Works

    From your screen to your door.

    1 · Design

    You build it here — piece, colorway, options, etch. The price you see is the price math we honor.

    2 · Proof

    At launch, every custom design gets a human proof check before production. Etch text and placement are confirmed by email.

    3 · Made on demand

    Your piece is produced per order by a vetted maker — glass finished and etched, apparel printed, gear assembled.

    4 · Shipped

    Production runs about 3–5 business days for apparel and gear, 5–10 for custom glass. Tracking lands in your inbox automatically.

    Studio FAQ

    Before you ask the budtender.

    Is my design saved anywhere besides this device?

    No. Saved designs live in this browser's local storage only. Use Copy Summary or Download Spec to keep a copy you can share or send us at order time.

    Why does taller glass cost more?

    More material, more breakage risk in production, and bigger packaging. The studio charges a flat $0.50 per centimeter over 30 cm — the same rule for every tall glass piece.

    Can I put anything in the etch text?

    Up to 14 characters. Names, initials, and short words etch best. At proof time we will flag anything that will not etch cleanly — and anything unlawful or hateful does not get made.

    When can I actually order?

    Checkout opens with the Ghost Marketplace launch. Until then this studio is a full design demo: no payment is taken here, ever. Join the drop list on the contact page to hear first.

    What if my piece arrives broken?

    Glass ships insured and padded like it owes us money. Breakage in transit gets replaced or refunded under the Marketplace terms that will be posted at launch — and the owner reads the support inbox personally.

    How do the 3D viewer, AR, and VR work?

    All three run in your browser. The 3D viewer (Three.js) rebuilds your design as a real-time model. AR exports that model so your phone can place it on a real shelf — it needs the live hosted site. The VR showroom (A-Frame/WebXR) is a walk-around space; on a headset, enter VR from inside the scene.

    What does no-inventory actually mean for my order?

    Your piece does not exist until you order it. Production starts at checkout — that is why custom lead times exist — and it is also why nothing here is ever out of stock, overstocked, or landfilled.

    Which pieces can be 3D printed?

    Most of the Gear Lab: grinders, trays, jars, dugouts, ashtrays, rolling machines, and stands. The Print Lab section above estimates weight, time, and price for each before you commit.

    What are Budz?

    The house loyalty currency from the GanjaGalaxy side of the platform. At launch, every dollar of build total earns 10 Budz toward member perks. The studio already counts them on your build summary.

    Ghost Pipeline

    Automatic procurement & dropshipping, end to end.

    1 · Order lands

    Checkout fires a production order containing the full build spec — piece, colorway, finish, etch, everything on this page.

    2 · Auto-procurement

    The engine picks the maker or supplier by rules: capability, current lead time, defect rate, shipping zone. No human touches it.

    3 · Made or pulled

    Custom work is produced per order; stock goods are dropshipped by the supplier under our label. Either way, we never held it.

    4 · Tracking & proof

    Carrier tracking flows back to the customer automatically; the maker uploads a photo proof before the box seals.

    Pro Care

    The 4-step glass reset.

    1 · Empty & rinse

    Warm water only. Never boiling water on cold glass — thermal shock cracks more pieces than drops do.

    2 · Iso + salt

    Isopropyl and coarse salt in, holes capped. The salt is the scrubber, the iso is the solvent.

    3 · Shake & soak

    Sixty seconds of shaking; stubborn spots soak twenty minutes. Pipe cleaners chase the percolator arms.

    4 · Rinse to silent

    Rinse until the water runs clear and quiet. A weekly light clean beats a monthly deep rescue, every time.
