Print on Demand

Print on demand means nothing is made until it is ordered. Your design is printed, pressed, or fabricated after checkout, so there is no warehouse of unsold stock and no waste from guessing what will sell.

How it works

You finish a design in the design studio, the store engine takes the order, and a production partner makes that one item to your specification. Apparel is printed or embroidered, accessories are finished to order, and 3D pieces are fabricated from the design file. Production time is part of delivery time, since making starts after you order.

Why it suits makers

Print on demand lets a maker offer many designs without paying for inventory up front. A design that sells can be reordered instantly, and a design that does not sell costs nothing but the time to make it. That makes it a low-risk way to test ideas with real buyers.

What to expect on quality and timing

Preview carefully, since the printed result follows the file you approve. Colors vary slightly between screens and physical production, and complex 3D designs may need adjustments for printability, which the studio flags before you order. Live checkout activates with the hemp-aware payment gateway. Until then, checkout pages show the flow honestly and take no payment.
