Logo Maker

The logo maker builds a brand mark for a hemp project without design training: pick a direction, adjust type and color, preview the result, and export a mark you can use across a shop, a channel, or a product line.

Start from a direction, not a blank page

Templates give a starting shape, and you shape it from there: a leaf mark, a word mark, or a badge. The strongest logos are simple, readable at small sizes, and recognizable in one color as well as full color.

Type, color, and spacing

Choose one strong typeface and one or two colors, and give the mark room to breathe. Crowded logos with many fonts and colors look busy at every size. Preview the mark on a dark background, a light background, and at thumbnail size before calling it done.

From logo to products

A finished logo flows into the design studio for apparel, stickers, and packaging, and into the marketplace for listing. Brand marks must be your own work or properly licensed; do not copy another brand's logo. Live checkout activates with the hemp-aware payment gateway.
