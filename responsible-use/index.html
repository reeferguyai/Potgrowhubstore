Responsible Use

Responsible use means enjoying hemp without creating problems for yourself or others. The rules below are simple, and following them keeps a legal product legal in practice.

Age and the law

You must be 18 or older to use this site, and the products featured here are hemp-derived only. Local laws vary on possession, use, and growing, so check the rules where you live and follow the strictest rule that applies to you.

Safe storage

Store hemp products in their original, labeled containers, in a cool, dark place, locked or out of reach of children and pets. Keep products clearly separate from anything a child might mistake for food, and never share products with anyone under the legal age.

Driving, work, and health

Do not drive or operate machinery after using any product that affects you, and follow your workplace rules on hemp and drug testing, since some products can trigger a positive test. For health questions, talk to a clinician rather than relying on general guides, and read the health disclaimer for the full limits of this site content.
