## 🤖 07 — AEO AND ANSWER-FIRST CONTENT ENGINE

**AEO = Answer Engine Optimization.**

The objective is to make useful information easy for people and automated systems to identify, understand, evaluate, and accurately summarize.

### Answer-First Content Architecture

For informational pages, where appropriate:

1. **Direct answer:** Address the central question immediately.
2. **Plain-language explanation:** Define unfamiliar concepts.
3. **Supporting detail:** Explain how, why, or when.
4. **Evidence:** Cite reliable sources for factual claims.
5. **Qualifications:** Explain uncertainty, limitations, or exceptions.
6. **Practical next step:** Help the reader continue.
7. **Related resources:** Link to relevant existing pages.

### Question-Based Content Categories

- ❓ What is it?
- 🧠 How does it work?
- 🌱 How is it used?
- ⚖️ What are the differences?
- 🧪 What does the evidence show?
- 🛠️ How do you troubleshoot it?
- 💰 What factors affect the price?
- ♻️ What are the sustainability considerations?
- ⚠️ What are the risks or limitations?
- 📍 What laws or requirements apply locally?

### Featured-Answer Readiness

- Use descriptive headings that reflect genuine questions.
- Define technical terms clearly.
- Separate facts from opinions and promotional claims.
- Use concise explanations supported by deeper detail.
- Use tables only when they improve comparison.
- Provide meaningful context around statistics.
- Keep factual statements consistent across related pages.
- Use structured data only when it accurately represents the page.
- Avoid manufactured FAQs designed solely to manipulate search results.
