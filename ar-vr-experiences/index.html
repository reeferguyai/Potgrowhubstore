AR and VR Experiences

Augmented and virtual reality turn flat pages into places you can walk through. In the ReeferDude world, that means virtual grow rooms you can plan in, and relaxation spaces you can unwind in, all from a browser where supported.

Virtual grow rooms

An AR and VR grow room lets you place lights, plants, and equipment in a virtual copy of your space before buying anything. Seeing the layout at real scale catches mistakes a flat sketch misses, such as a light hung too low or a walkway that disappears once plants fill in.

Relaxation and community spaces

Virtual relaxation spaces, including Chillout VR, are for downtime and community hangouts: listening, talking, and unwinding between grows. The same community guidelines apply inside virtual spaces as everywhere else on the site.

What you need to join

These experiences run in the browser where supported, with headset support where your device offers it. Text-based alternatives, including the guides, the budtender, and community forums, remain available for anyone who cannot use a 3D space.
