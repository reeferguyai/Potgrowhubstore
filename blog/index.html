Blog

The blog is where ReeferDude writes at length: growing explainers, strain profiles, product explainers, and news about the platform and the hemp world. Articles are written to teach, not to sell, and every claim worth checking links to a source or a lab page.

Growing articles

Growing articles go deeper than the quick guides: side-by-side comparisons of setups, troubleshooting series for common plant problems, and seasonal notes tied to the grow calendar. Each article links back to the glossary so no term blocks understanding.

Strain and product articles

Strain articles profile families and terpene characters, while product articles explain product types, labels, and lab reports. These are educational profiles, not listings: they help you understand what to look for, and the shop pages handle buying separately.

News and updates

News articles cover platform launches and changes, industry developments, and regulatory explainers, including the federal hemp rules taking effect December 11, 2026. Subscribe through the newsletter so new articles reach you as they publish.
