Our Values: Straight Talk, Fair Deals

Values pages are usually wallpaper. This one is a checklist we can be held to — each value below is a practice with a page that proves it, not a poster. If you catch us breaking one, Report a Problem is the door and the changelog is where the correction will show.

Honesty in commerce

Real prices or clearly-labelled demo prices. No fake crossed-out anchors, no invented stock counts, no 'only 2 left' fiction. Seconds are graded and described. Mistakes in listings get corrected in public, in the changelog.

Honesty in knowledge

Guides cite what is known, label what is folk wisdom, and correct themselves — the fact-check and sources page exists so you can audit any claim we make. Medical claims are never made for products; health content is education with a disclaimer, always.

Law and care

Age gates are real gates, legal guides push you to verify locally, harm reduction is treated as core content rather than a footnote. The shop would rather lose a sale than break a law or hurt a person — that ordering is not negotiable.
