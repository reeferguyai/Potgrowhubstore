About us

One hustle. One ecosystem.

PotGrowHub is a founder-built, culture-first hub for cannabis education, grow guidance, creative tools, and honest product discovery — built by hand, on a real bench, with zero fluff.

The founders

ReeferDude and PotGrowHub were founded by Ray Meadows and his wife — a two-person founding team. Ray brings the bench: years as a repair technician for phones, computers, and devices, a lifetime of sales instinct, and a grower's obsession with the details. After major heart surgery in 2019, he taught himself the modern tech stack piece by piece — and this platform is what came out the other side: part head shop, part grow school, part AI lab.

The philosophy is simple and it doesn't change: education first, responsible use always, and straight answers over hype. If something on this site is a demo, it says so. If we don't know, we say that too.

The family of brands

  • PotGrowHub.store — the roof. The hub everything lives under.
  • ReeferDude — the persona and front door. The gangster-hippie guide who walks you in.
  • GanjaGoddessAI — the brains. Our voice-controlled AI virtual budtender.
  • GanjaGuru — the blueprint. The long-term platform plan everything here is growing into: ghost commerce, design and manufacturing studios, VR grow rooms, and a gamified galaxy of growers.

Where it's going

Now: education, the Strain Creation Lab, the Blueprinting Studio, design demos, and consultations. Next: the Ghost Marketplace opens — zero-inventory, agent-run commerce with print-on-demand and eco-first sourcing. On the horizon: 3D and AR/VR grow-room design, deeper AI, and the GanjaGalaxy community layer.

The tagline on the wall says it all: "Let AI Grow It, You Smoke It."
