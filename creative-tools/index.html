Creative Tools

Creative tools turn ideas into things people can hold: custom artwork, logos, stickers, apparel, and 3D pieces. This page maps the tools, what each one is for, and how a finished design reaches the store.

Design tools at a glance

The design studio is the main workspace for accessories, apparel, and 3D pieces, with live previews before you commit. The logo maker builds brand marks, and the sticker designer turns artwork into stickers. Each tool previews the result so you see problems before production starts.

From idea to finished piece

Start from a blank or a template, add artwork and text, pick colors and materials, and preview the result. The tool flags designs that may not print well, such as tiny text or thin lines, so you can fix them early. Finished designs move to the store engine for production and delivery.

Sharing and reordering

Design files stay attached to your account, so you can reorder a favorite, remix an old design, or share work with the community. Makers can also list finished pieces on the marketplace under the seller terms. Live checkout activates with the hemp-aware payment gateway. Until then, checkout pages show the flow honestly and take no payment.
