Dosing Calculator: Start Low, With Numbers

Most dosing disasters are arithmetic failures: a percentage nobody converted, a serving nobody divided. This calculator does the small math that matters — product weight and potency in, milligrams and a conservative starting dose out — for edibles, flower and concentrates, with tolerance bands that adjust the answer honestly.

How it works

Enter the product's THC percentage (or total mg for edibles), what you have, and your experience band — new, occasional, regular. The calculator returns total THC, per-serving math for your chosen portion, and the porch-standard starting point for your band, with the wait times printed in the same size type as the numbers.

The non-negotiables

Edibles: wait two full hours before more. Concentrates: the starting unit is a grain of rice. New users start at the calculator's floor, not its middle. These rules print on every result because the calculator's job is not to get you highest — it is to get you home happy.

Tolerance honesty

Tolerance bands are self-reported and the calculator says so. If your band has crept — doses climbing, effects shrinking — the wellness page's tolerance break guide is the companion read, offered without judgment.
