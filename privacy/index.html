Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who we are

ReeferDude.com and PotGrowHub.store are operated by the same owner ("we", "us"). This page explains what information is collected when you visit, and what is done with it.

What we collect

Hosting logs. This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may log your IP address, browser type, and pages requested to operate and secure the service.

Analytics and advertising. We use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense. These services set cookies and collect usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type) to measure traffic and serve ads. Google controls how it processes that data under its own privacy policy.

On-device storage. Features such as the AI Budtender chat may store your conversation history and preferences in your own browser's local storage. That data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time from the chat history panel or your browser settings.

Messages you send us. If you contact us by email or a linked form, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to respond.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not require an account to browse this site. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21 — this site is intended for adults of legal age in their jurisdiction.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser at any time; the site will still load, though some features may not remember your preferences. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party links

This site links to other websites and marketplaces. Their privacy practices are their own; this policy covers only ReeferDude.com and PotGrowHub.store.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
