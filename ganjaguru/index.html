GanjaGuru

GanjaGuru is the internal blueprint for the ReeferDude platform: the plan for how learning, tools, community, and the store fit together as one experience rather than a pile of separate pages.

The platform idea

The blueprint imagines the platform as a living space instead of a filing cabinet: a central home with distinct areas for growing, making, selling, relaxing, and learning. A visitor starts at the center and moves to the area that matches what they want to do, rather than guessing at menu labels.

The areas of the platform

Grow areas cover cultivation guides, planners, and the grow room designer. Maker areas cover the design studio and print-on-demand. Commerce areas cover the shop and marketplace, powered by the PotGrowHub store engine. Community areas cover forums, events, and relaxation spaces such as Chillout VR.

What the blueprint means for visitors

For visitors, the blueprint shows up as consistency: the same guides behind the tools, the same community rules everywhere, and the same honest limits on shopping and checkout. The flagship pages on this site are the public face of that plan, built one slice at a time, starting with the landing page and the guides.
