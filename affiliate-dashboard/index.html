Affiliate Program: Share The Porch, Earn Straight

Affiliates are the porch's word of mouth, formalised: share your link, earn a stated percentage on resulting orders, watch it all in a dashboard that shows clicks, orders and earnings in real time. This page is both the sign-up and the rulebook — read the rules before the rate excites you.

The deal

Commission percentage and cookie window are printed here at launch and in your dashboard permanently — changes apply prospectively with 30 days notice, never retroactively. Payouts monthly above a low threshold, by the payment methods on the payment options page.

The rules

Disclose the relationship everywhere (the affiliate disclosure page is the standard). No bidding on the shop's own brand terms, no spam, no medical claims, no marketing to minors, ever — violations end the account and unpaid commissions on the violating traffic. The culture is the brand; affiliates who spend it do not stay.
