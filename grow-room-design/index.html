Grow Room Design

A good grow room is designed before anything is bought. Space, light, airflow, and workflow decide most of your results, and fixing a bad layout after plants are in is much harder than planning it right the first time.

Start with the space you have

Measure the room, note ceiling height, doorways, and power outlets, and decide how many plants the space can hold at full size, not as seedlings. Leave working room on at least one side of every plant. A crowded room grows mold faster than it grows plants.

Light layout and airflow

Plan light coverage so every plant gets even light, with fixtures you can raise as plants grow. Airflow matters as much as light: fresh air in, stale air out, and gentle movement inside so no pocket of still, humid air sits on leaves. The grow lights and environment pages go deeper on both topics.

Workflow: water, work, and harvest

Design for the jobs you will do every week. Put water access near the plants, keep tools and nutrients in one spot, and leave a clear path for moving plants at harvest. The grow room planner page helps you sketch a layout before you build it.
