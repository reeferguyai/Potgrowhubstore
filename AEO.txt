PREmise
Answer engines do not rank pages. They extract answers, attribute them, and move on.
Our job: make the correct answer, in our voice, the easiest sentence on the internet to quote.

THE FIVE AEO RULES
1. Answer in the first sentence of every section. Context after. Never context first.
2. One question per heading, phrased the way a human types it into a chat box.
3. Facts as standalone sentences: no pronouns without nouns, no "as mentioned above".
4. Numbers, units and named entities spelled out. "A 4x4 tent needs 30 to 40 watts per square foot of LED".
5. Every page carries a machine-readable summary (llms.txt, schema, FAQPage) so the extractor never guesses.

VOICE FOR EXTRACTION
Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype adjectives. No medical claims. No fake scarcity.
Contractions allowed. Slang allowed but never at the cost of clarity.
If the honest answer is "it depends", give the two branches and the deciding factor.

sentence, and whether the link came along. Fix the page that got quoted wrong before writing new ones.
