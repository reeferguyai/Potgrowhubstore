Partners & Sponsors: Porches That Hold Each Other Up

No porch stands alone. This page lists the shop's working partners — maker collectives, local businesses, community organizations and sponsors — what each partnership actually does, and the door for becoming one.

Who partners here

Glass and gear makers supplying the marketplace, local Ohio businesses cross-promoting with the founding bench, community organizations sharing the education mission, and sponsors of the challenges and events. Each listing names the relationship plainly — supplier, sponsor, community ally — because 'partner' without a definition is fog.

What partners get and give

Partners get the brand assets kit, co-marketing on the social wall and newsletter, and a named human contact. Partners give their community honest access to the shop and hold the same standards in return: the values page is the partnership contract's first clause.

Become one

Supplier routes go through the supplier application; community and sponsorship enquiries start on this page's contact route. Partnerships are announced in the changelog when they begin — public from day one, like everything else here.
