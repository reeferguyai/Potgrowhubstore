Strain Library

The strain library is the organized collection of hemp strain profiles on ReeferDude, grouped by family, aroma, and growing traits so you can compare strains instead of memorizing names.

Browse by family

Families give a first filter: indica-leaning plants tend to stay shorter and bushier, sativa-leaning plants tend to grow taller with longer flowering times, and hybrids mix traits from both sides. Family is a starting point, not a promise, because individual strains and growing conditions vary.

Browse by aroma and terpene

Aroma is often a better guide than family. Pine, citrus, earth, and spice notes point to different terpene profiles, and learning the main terpenes helps you describe what you like and find strains with a similar character.

Compare growing traits

Each profile notes growth pattern, flowering time, and plant size where known, so you can match a strain to your space before committing. Use the strain builder to turn a shortlist into a grow plan, and the strain index for the full alphabetical list.
