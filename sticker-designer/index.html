Sticker Designer

The sticker designer turns artwork into stickers: upload or build a design, pick a shape and size, preview the result, and send it to print-on-demand production.

Designing a sticker that prints well

Bold shapes, clear outlines, and readable text print best. Tiny text and hairline details disappear at sticker size, so preview at actual size before ordering. A clean border around the artwork helps the cut line and keeps the design looking finished.

Shapes, sizes, and finishes

Die-cut stickers follow the artwork shape, while standard shapes suit badges and labels. Sizes range from small laptop stickers to large display pieces. Finishes change the feel: matte for a soft look, gloss for bright color, and weather-resistant finishes for outdoor use.

From design to delivery

Finished designs move to the store engine for production and delivery, with nothing printed until it is ordered. Designs stay attached to your account for reordering or remixing. Live checkout activates with the hemp-aware payment gateway. Until then, checkout pages show the flow honestly and take no payment.
