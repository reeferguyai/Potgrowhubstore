Cultivation

Cultivation is the whole job of growing hemp well: starting plants, feeding and watering them, watching their health, and bringing them to harvest in good condition. This page gives the overview, and the linked guides go deep on each stage.

The hemp life cycle

Hemp grows from seed to seedling, then through vegetative growth, flowering, and harvest. Each stage wants different light hours, water, and food. Seedlings are fragile and need gentle handling, vegetative plants want steady growth conditions, and flowering plants need careful monitoring as buds form and mature.

What the plant needs

Every stage comes back to the same basics: light, air, water, nutrients, and a stable environment. Get those right and most problems stay small. Get one badly wrong, such as drowning roots or starving plants, and growth stalls while you fix it.

Growing legally and responsibly

Before growing anything, check your local laws on hemp cultivation, plant counts, and licensing. Rules vary by place and change over time. Responsible cultivation also means safe storage, keeping plants away from children, and following the responsible use page.
