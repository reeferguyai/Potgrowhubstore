Our commitment

Accessibility

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Our goal

We want every adult visitor to be able to use this site. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we treat accessibility as ongoing work, not a one-time checkbox.

What is built in

  • A "Skip to content" link on every page for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Semantic HTML structure with labeled navigation and descriptive link text
  • Alternative text on meaningful images and icons
  • Color combinations chosen for readable contrast on the dark theme
  • Visible keyboard focus states, and support for reduced-motion preferences
  • A layout that adapts to small screens and zoomed views without horizontal scrolling
  • Voice input and spoken replies in the AI Budtender where the browser supports them

Known limits

Some third-party content we link to or embed (marketplaces, ad networks, video hosts) is outside our control and may not meet the same standard. If an embedded feature ever blocks you, the underlying information is also available in plain text elsewhere on the site or by contacting us.

Tell us what is broken

If any part of this site is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us through the contact page. Include the page address and what went wrong; we will work to fix it and, where we can, provide the content another way in the meantime.
