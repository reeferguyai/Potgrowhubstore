Voice & Chat Help: Talking To Ray

Ray is the porch's AI budtender: strain guidance, gear advice, grow basics, order help — awake always, honest about being software. This page explains how to get the best from him: typing or voice, the questions he is great at, and the lines he will not cross.

Talking to Ray

Type like you talk. Voice input works anywhere you see the microphone — speak plainly, name the strain or product if you know it, and correct him freely: 'no, the other Blue Dream question' genuinely helps. He remembers the conversation you are in, not your life — each new chat starts fresh by design.

What Ray knows

The full catalog with specs, the strain library and terpene data, every guide on this site, order and policy pages — he reads the same pages you do and cites them. What he does not know: your account, your orders, anything requiring your private data — those route to human support with your order number.

The lines

Ray will not diagnose medical conditions, will not help break the law where you live, and will not pretend to be human. He will happily explain the law, teach the science and find you the guide — the porch rules apply to the porch's AI first.
