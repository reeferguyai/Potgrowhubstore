Pest Management

Pest management starts with prevention. Healthy plants in a clean, well-ventilated space resist pests far better than stressed plants in a dirty one, and catching a problem early keeps it small.

Prevention first

Keep the grow space clean, remove dead leaves promptly, avoid bringing outdoor plants or unwashed tools inside, and inspect new plants in isolation before adding them. Good airflow and sensible humidity also deny pests the still, damp conditions many of them prefer.

Common pests and what they look like

Spider mites leave fine webbing and speckled leaves. Aphids cluster on stems and new growth and leave sticky residue. Fungus gnats hover over damp medium and indicate overwatering. Thrips leave silvery streaks on leaves. Identify the pest before treating, since the wrong treatment wastes time while the population grows.

Safe control options

Start with physical controls: removing affected leaves, washing plants gently, and using traps for flying insects. Biological controls, such as predatory insects, suit growers who want to avoid sprays. Any spray product must be legal for hemp in your area and safe for the growth stage; never spray anything unapproved on plants near harvest.
